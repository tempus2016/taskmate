# Chore & Reward Library

*(v7.3.0)* The library is a ready-made list of 199 chores in 12 categories and 45 rewards, plus the six built-in [template packs](Template-Packs.md). Tick what suits your family and add it all in one go. Every name is translated into every language TaskMate supports.

---

## Opening It

Click **📚 Library** on the toolbar of the **Chores** or **Rewards** page in the [Admin Panel](Admin-Panel.md). The tabs at the top switch between chores and rewards.

It also opens from:

- **The Setup Wizard.** The chores and rewards steps have a **Browse the whole library** link. What you pick joins the wizard's list and is created with everything else on Review. The wizard's own 32 suggestions are the library's top picks. See [Setup Wizard](Setup-Wizard.md).
- **A birthday.** When a child's birthday brings new chores into reach, the nudge's **See chore ideas** opens the library on *New for {name}*, the chores that suit them now.

---

## Finding Chores

| Control | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Suits** | Pick the children you're choosing for, or **Everyone**. Chores are filtered by each child's age (from their birthday, otherwise the age group picked in the setup wizard). [Adults](Adult-Profiles.md) and guests never appear here |
| **Search** | Matches names in your language: *dog*, *laundry*, *homework* |
| **Hide ones we already have** | Leaves out anything you already have: added from the library, or with the same name |
| **Categories** | For chores: **Top picks**, **Packs**, then Morning, School & homework, Kitchen & cooking, Tidying & cleaning, Laundry, House & garden, Pets, Responsibility, Health & exercise, Family & kindness, Evening and Learning & hobbies, each with a count |

Each chore shows its suitable ages (*age 6+*, *age 4–8*). Something you already have is marked **Added**. A chore added from the library keeps that mark even after you rename it.

---

## Adding

1. Tick the chores or rewards you want.
2. **Customise** one to change this copy before it's added: the name, points (or cost, for a reward), time of day and which days it repeats. No days ticked means every day. You can still edit it afterwards like any other chore.
3. Under **For**, choose who each chore is for. The children it suits are ticked already.
4. **Add (5)** adds everything at once.

Anything you already have is skipped, and the message says how many were added and how many skipped.

### Packs

The **Packs** category holds the six built-in sets: Morning routine, Evening routine, Kitchen helper, Weekend helper, Pet care, and Homework & reading. Ticking a pack selects all its chores and skips the ones you already have.

Chores you saved yourself as templates stay on the [Templates](Template-Packs.md) page.

---

## Good to Know

- **Rewards keep a fixed cost**: the one shown, or the one you type in Customise.
- Only Home Assistant **admins** can add from the library. Each add goes into the [audit log](Admin-Audit-Log.md) as a library add.
- Chores and rewards remember which library entry they came from. That survives a [backup and restore](Backup-and-Restore.md), so *Added* still shows afterwards.
